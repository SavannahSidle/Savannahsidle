Current advocacy

Fix Gaps in Ontario’s Long-Term Care: Protect Mobility, Dignity, & Quality of Life

Savannah began this petition in 2024 after her mother, who has early-onset Alzheimer’s disease, struggled to access appropriate long-term care while under age 65. After sustained advocacy, her mother secured a placement at Forest Hill Long-Term Care.

The campaign expanded in 2026 after her mother’s mobility declined following a hip fracture. She can still walk with support. Limited staffing restricts her access to supervised walking, rehabilitation, and time outside her wheelchair.

The petition calls on the Ontario government and Ministry of Long-Term Care to increase staffing and rehabilitation capacity, improve access to physiotherapy and occupational therapy, provide meaningful mobility support, base care plans on residents’ functional needs, address gaps affecting younger people with dementia, and support families as partners in care.
