Current advocacy

Fix Gaps in Ontario’s Long-Term Care: Protect Mobility, Dignity, & Quality of Life

The petition began in 2024 after Savannah’s mother, who has early-onset Alzheimer’s disease, struggled to access appropriate long-term care while under age 65. After sustained advocacy, a placement was secured at Forest Hill Long-Term Care.

The campaign expanded in 2026 after a hip fracture affected mobility. Savannah’s mother can still walk with support. Limited staffing restricts access to supervised walking, rehabilitation, and time outside the wheelchair.

The petition calls on the Ontario government and Ministry of Long-Term Care to increase staffing and rehabilitation capacity, improve access to physiotherapy and occupational therapy, provide meaningful mobility support, base care plans on residents’ functional needs, address gaps affecting younger people with dementia, and support families as partners in care.
