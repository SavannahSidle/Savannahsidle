Human–AI interaction and research

Master’s research · Human–AI interaction

User Trust of Adaptive Self-Aware Interfaces

A mixed-methods study of trust, comfort, and perceived safety when interfaces detect changing conditions and adapt autonomously. Scenarios examined unfamiliar networks, degraded connectivity, extended absence, and proactive system intervention.

  • Trust calibration
  • Adaptive systems
  • Mixed methods
  • Human factors

Research framework

Embodied Adaptive Interface Framework

A conceptual framework for interfaces that perceive relevant environmental conditions, adapt behaviour, and communicate context-sensitive responses. The framework connects HCI, embodied cognition, psychology, and adaptive systems.
